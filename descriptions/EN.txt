[h2]What it is[/h2]
The SpiffBoy SDK is the development kit of the [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3825220364]SpiffBoy[/url] handheld console. Like the kits console makers gave to game studios, it gives you everything you need to make your own game cartridges: example cartridges to learn from, a developer menu, documentation, tools and test robots.

This mod requires SpiffBoy.

[h2]What is in the mod[/h2]
- Hello SpiffBoy: the smallest complete cartridge. Spiffo moves with the arrows, says hello with A, plays a short tune, saves your best score and shows translated texts. Its code is commented line by line, and it comes with practice exercises and their solutions.
- Spiff-Pong: a complete cartridge. Title screen, menus, a match against the console or against a friend on the link cable, music and sound effects on the sound chip, best scores with initials, and options. Its code explains how a full game is built, including how two consoles stay in sync on the cable.
- The SpiffBoy SDK menu: right-click the world and choose SpiffBoy SDK. It gives you a console, batteries, headphones, earbuds, the link cable, and every cartridge installed in your game, from SpiffBoy, from the SDK or from any other mod, alone or already inserted in a console. In multiplayer it is reserved to players allowed to add items, and the server checks every request.

The example cartridges are never found as loot: you get them from the SDK menu.

[h2]What is in the kit[/h2]
The full kit comes inside the mod itself, in its SDK folder: mods/SpiffBoySDK/SDK
- Documentation, step by step: getting started, how a cartridge works, the screen and its four shades, the sound chip and its music notation, saving, texts and translations, the link cable, the cartridge item and its 3D model, testing, the pitfalls of the game's Lua, publishing.
- Tools: turn your art into sprites for the screen, make the 3D model, label and inventory icon of your cartridge, check your texts in every language, and create a new cartridge from the example in one command.
- Test robots: play your cartridge outside the game, thousands of frames in a few seconds, to catch crashes, missing texts and frames too heavy to draw.

[h2]Q&A[/h2]
Q: Do I need this mod to play SpiffBoy?
A: No. SpiffBoy works on its own. The SDK is for players who want to make their own games, or to try the example cartridges.

Q: Can my cartridges live in my own mod?
A: Yes, that is the whole point. Your mod requires SpiffBoy, registers its cartridges, and ships its own art, texts and music. The console finds everything in your mod.

Q: Is it safe to add or remove mid-save?
A: Yes. The SDK saves nothing in your world. Removing it only removes the example cartridges and the menu.
